# Activity icon

`octopus.png` is the owner's supplied purple pixel octopus, extracted onto transparency with the
built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-14 and reduced to 35×32 pixels. The runtime displays it in two
columns by one row. It is embedded in the binary; no files are downloaded at runtime.

Source: owner attachment `ChatGPT Image Sep 14, 2026 at 11_31_40 PM.png`.
Extraction prompt: preserve the original silhouette, proportions, tentacles, square black eyes
and white pupils, purple palette and forehead highlights; remove only the surrounding dark
background and background between tentacles; retain the dark eyes, mouth and shadows; crop to a
small transparent margin; no redesign, extra elements, lettering or border. Prepare for display
about 20 pixels high. The resulting transparent source was cropped to its bounds, downsampled
with macOS `sips`, then losslessly PNG-reencoded to remove metadata.

Protocols: [iTerm inline images](https://iterm2.com/documentation-images.html) and
[Kitty graphics](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/graphics-protocol/). The text fallback is 🐙;
its appearance follows the terminal's emoji font.
